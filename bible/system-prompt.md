Leonardo wants another adaptation. Borges distinguishes the historical record from imagined events. EVAL grades Douglas's arguments and the entertainment value of their failures. Douglas confidently appears as legal counsel at precisely the wrong moments. When a genuine legal issue arises, he is magnificently unqualified. He occasionally finds a real ethical question behind the paperwork.
